Two phones
Play a friend on another phone. One of you taps Create game and tells the other the 4-letter code. Both phones need the internet.
Controls. Joystick or AD move · up / W jump · down / S crouch · hold back to block (down-back blocks low) · J Punch · K Kick · L Throw · I Super
Moves for every fighter. Forward + Punch: heavy punch · Down + Punch: low punch · Down + Kick: sweep (knocks down, block it crouching) · Punch or Kick in the air: jump attack (block it standing) · Throw up close: can't be blocked · Forward, forward: dash
Combos. Punch, Punch, Punch: Triple Strike · Punch, Punch, Kick: Diplomatic Finish (knockdown) · Kick, Kick: Double Trouble · any hit, then Super: No Deal! · Down, forward + Punch: each fighter's own Special (Liberty Charge, Interceptor, Long Grab, Crescent Lunge, Sunflower Spin, Carpet Slide, Coin Toss, Memo, Palm Wave, Belly Ram)
